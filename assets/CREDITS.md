primitives in `scripts/audio/dsp.py`, adapted from the Spaceballs / openjar-launcher cue tools).
Seeded per stem, reproducible; no samples, recordings, voices or third-party material. The
per-file list and what each sounds like is in `assets/audio/CUES.md`.
