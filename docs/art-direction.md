# Junk Yard Zap: art direction

From the plan (Kaden, 2026-11-09), adjusted for the Jarlings:

- **A toy diorama:** a sunny junk yard seen from the side like a model on a table. One key light, soft shadows, a painted sky with parallax clouds, and the camera slightly above eye level.
- **Characters:** Jeff (P1, green) and Chuck (P2, red) as **Jarlings** from `openjar-characters`, unchanged from Spaceballs. Guns and the gun belt are equipment gear on their slots.
- **Objects:** chunky, slightly too big, built from junk. Each one has a simple face (two dots and a mouth) that reacts: wide-eyed when grabbed, dizzy when thrown, smug when bouncy, panicked when ballooning.
- **Technique:** to be confirmed with Kaden before any Art lane. The plan says low-poly flat-shaded with outlines; Spaceballs uses the smoother, Wii-Sports-like Jarling look. Recommendation: the same smooth Jarling look as Spaceballs, so the two games feel like one library.
- **Palette:** three proposals; Kaden picks. Junk is rusty and warm, the sky and pond are cool, and each gun has its own saturated colour that is never the players' green `#3cc24a` or red `#e4393c`.
- **Original designs only. No real places or personal landmarks.**
